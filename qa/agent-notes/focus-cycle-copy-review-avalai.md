### 1. Terminology Critique

* **"Focus Cycle"**: **Strong recommendation.** It accurately describes a bounded, repeating learning structure without military, gaming, or marketing connotations. "Cycle" signals a structured timeframe (e.g., a 5-day rhythm), while "Focus" highlights target language attributes.
* **"Clinical Precision" vs. "Fluent Recall"**: **Recommended with clear linguistic framing.** 
  * *Clinical Precision* clearly targets exact register, collocations, and low-error phrasing. To prevent confusion with medical diagnostic skill, all supporting copy must explicitly refer to *language* and *expression*.
  * *Fluent Recall* clearly targets retrieval speed, vocabulary breadth, and adaptation across communication formats.

---

### 2. Recommended Terminology System

* **Core Structure**: Focus Cycle
* **Option A**: Clinical Precision *(Precision-oriented)*
* **Option B**: Fluent Recall *(Fluency-oriented)*
* **Milestones**: Goals (3 per cycle)
* **Progress Tracking**: Journey & XP

### 4. Compliance & Risk Flags

1. **Medical Competence Risk**: Words like "Clinical Precision" must never stand alone without context referring to *language, register, or phrasing*. Avoid phrases like "Clinical Mastery" or "Precision Care Language" which imply medical skill testing.
2. **Unsupported Future Promises**: Avoid UI text referencing "Cycle 2" or "Upcoming Cycles" until dynamic cycle generation is deployed. Refer only to "next focus" or "new cycle."
3. **No Patient Outcomes**: Avoid framing exercises around "improving patient outcomes" or "reducing clinical error." Frame all benefits around "clear communication," "accurate phrasing," and "durable recall."
