# Cinnamon practice feedback and recap copy brief

Review English microcopy for an adult medical-English learning app. Return one recommended line per slot, followed by at most one short rationale. Keep it concise, warm, mature, and honest. Completion may create one verified practice event, but these mini-games do not certify clinical competence or fluency.

Constraints:

- No shame, streak threat, casino language, or exaggerated praise.
- Wrong answers are retryable learning information.
- Do not mention databases or implementation details.
- Screen-reader announcements must be short.
- Plain international English.

Slots:

1. Sentence Unscramble helper shown while not all words have been placed.
2. Sentence Unscramble incorrect-order feedback.
3. Sentence Unscramble accepted-sentence feedback with placeholders `{current}` and `{total}`.
4. Sentence Unscramble completion title.
5. Sentence Unscramble completion detail with `{total}`.
6. Vocab Match completion title to replace the childish `You Win!`.
7. Vocab Match timeout title and one short helper line that makes clear the round can be tried again without penalty.
8. A neutral CTA label that exits a completed practice recap.
