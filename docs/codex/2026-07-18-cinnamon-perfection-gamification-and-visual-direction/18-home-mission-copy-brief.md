# Cinnamon home mission copy brief

Review compact English copy for a real daily mission card in an adult medical-English learning app.

Behavior:

- A quest is assigned only when at least three due words exist.
- Progress counts three distinct successful due reviews from the immutable local ledger.
- Completion exposes a manual, one-time `+10 XP` claim; retry cannot duplicate value.
- Before assignment, the card sends the learner to choose enough words for the queue.
- If the queue is temporarily empty after assignment, the button is disabled rather than fabricating progress.

Current copy by state:

- Eyebrow: `TODAY’S MISSION`
- Unassigned title: `Prime today’s quest`
- Unassigned detail: `Choose N more words, then complete three distinct reviews.`
- In progress CTA: `Review next`
- Empty assigned CTA: `Queue updated`
- Complete title/detail: `Quest complete` / `Your verified reviews are recorded. Claim the one-time reward.`
- Claim CTA: `Claim +10 XP`
- Claimed title/detail/state: `Quest cleared` / `10 XP is settled once. This checkpoint stays complete.` / `Reward secured`
- Progress: `N / 3 verified`

Task: return improved final candidates only. Tone: vivid, warm, motivating, mature—not dry, childish, casino-like, urgent, or shaming. Preserve exact product truth. Titles max 4 words, CTAs max 3 words excluding `+10 XP`, details max 14 words.
