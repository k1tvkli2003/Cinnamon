# Reward presentation copy brief

You are a concise senior UX writer helping with a language-learning Android app.
Return final answer only; do not include hidden reasoning.

Context:

- The app records verified learning actions in an immutable local ledger. It can truthfully show a reward only after the record is saved.
- A reward currently appears as a short snackbar: `+{xp} XP secured in your learning ledger` or `Learning progress recorded`.
- The replacement is a small, dismissible in-app reward card. It is shown after a real review or a completed practice session, may appear several times per session, and must work with a screen reader and reduced motion.
- The audience is adult language learners, including people practicing clinical English. The product should feel warm, capable, and motivating—not childish, arcade-like, pressuring, or falsely celebratory.
- Do not imply grades, fluency certification, streak pressure, leaderboards, or server/cloud sync. Do not claim an XP award when the amount is zero.
- Space: a 1-line title (max 32 English characters), a 1-line detail (max 62 characters), and an explicit accessible dismiss label (max 24 characters).

Task:

Offer 6 compact English copy directions for the reward card. Each direction must give a title for a positive XP reward, a title for zero XP / saved progress, a detail template using `{xp}` only when positive, and an accessible dismiss label. Keep wording factual and proportional. Prefer sentence case.
