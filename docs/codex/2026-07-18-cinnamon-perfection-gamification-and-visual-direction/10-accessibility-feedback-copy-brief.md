# Cinnamon accessibility and game-feedback copy brief

Review only the English microcopy below for an adult medical-English learning app. Return one recommended line per slot, followed by at most one short rationale. The product must feel motivating and alive without pretending to assess clinical competence, fluency, or patient outcomes.

Constraints:

- Calm, direct, mature, and encouraging; never childish, shaming, or casino-like.
- Screen-reader announcements should be short enough to hear repeatedly.
- Do not mention hidden implementation details, databases, or internal scoring.
- A mismatch is neutral information, not failure.
- A timed round pauses when the app leaves the foreground; do not imply punishment.
- Sound, haptics, and reduced motion are accessibility/presentation preferences only and never change learning rewards.
- Use plain international English.

Slots:

1. Vocab Match, first card selected: a short screen-reader state.
2. Vocab Match, pair matched: a short live announcement.
3. Vocab Match, pair mismatched: a short live announcement that suggests trying another card.
4. Vocab Match, timer paused because the app is not active: a short state announcement.
5. Profile setting label for disabling nonessential interface sounds.
6. Profile setting label for disabling nonessential haptic feedback.
7. Profile setting label for reducing decorative motion.
8. One short helper sentence explaining that these three settings do not alter progress or rewards.

Do not propose celebratory copy tied to a specific mascot or visual world; that identity has already been chosen by the user but its identifier is not available in this execution context.
